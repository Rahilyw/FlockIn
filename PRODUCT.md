# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University students discovering and engaging with campus life. They open FlockIn between classes, on mobile, in bright campus environments, with a quick-browse mindset. Primary job: find something worth going to today.

## Product Purpose

Centralize campus discovery. Events, clubs, and resources in one place, surfaced by interest and activity. Success means a student finds something they care about without effort. Tagline: "Your Campus Connected."

## Brand Personality

Warm, energetic, inviting. Three words: alive, approachable, electric. The interface should feel like a well-designed physical noticeboard that buzzes with real activity, not a corporate portal.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS: bland blue-and-white, Intercom-style feature grids, soulless corporate palette.
- Dark admin dashboards: heavy sidebar nav, dense data tables, IT-tool vibes.
- Social media feeds: infinite scroll, engagement counters, algorithmic content pressure.
- Overstuffed student portals: every feature crammed onto one screen, zero visual hierarchy, university intranet energy.

## Design Principles

1. **Energy without chaos.** The platform is lively but never overwhelming. Motion and color serve excitement, not distraction.
2. **Physical world as metaphor.** The noticeboard, pushpins, and posters are load-bearing identity. Lean into tactile, real-world references.
3. **Fast to value.** A student should find something worth joining in under 30 seconds. Hierarchy serves discovery speed.
4. **Warmth at every touchpoint.** Terracotta, pastel blobs, Montserrat: the brand palette is already set. Polish reinforces it, doesn't replace it.
5. **Consistent without being boring.** Same system, varied rhythm. Spacing, sizing, and weight create variety within coherence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast as a floor. Reduced motion support already in place (respect `prefers-reduced-motion`). Semantic HTML via shadcn/Radix. Touch targets minimum 44x44px on mobile.
